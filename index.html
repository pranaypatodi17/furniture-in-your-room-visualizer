Why scale-aware

Pictures are easy. Fit is what matters.

True-to-size overlay

Furniture is drawn using its real dimensions against the room width you provide, not just stretched to look nice.

Clearance checks

Gaps to side walls, the back wall and the walkway in front are calculated and flagged when they look too tight.

Honest estimates

A single photo can't give exact measurements. We say so, and let you adjust room dimensions yourself.

Fewer returns

Customers who see size and clearance up front buy with more confidence. See the retailer view for sample metrics.

Place furniture in your room

Simulated preview. Furniture is overlaid as a flat illustration at an estimated scale. It is not an AI render and is not a precise measurement.

1 · Choose a room

Or upload your own room photo JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB. The photo stays in your browser.

2 · Preview

Add several pieces: each product you click is added to the room. Click a piece to select it, drag to move, use the corner handle to resize. With a piece focused: arrow keys move, + / − resize, [ and ] rotate, Delete removes.

3 · Scale & dimension check

Select furniture
Room dimensions (adjust for accuracy)

Estimates only. Scale is derived from the wall width you enter, assuming the photo spans that wall straight-on. A photo alone can't give exact measurements. For better accuracy, measure a wall (or a known object like a door, 203 cm) and enter it.
